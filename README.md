# Chalk Talk

A play you sketch or say on your phone becomes an animated, step-by-step play on the living-room TV, with "the read" explained at every beat and live "what if they switch?" re-plans. Built for volunteer youth basketball coaches. Entered in the Build, Ship, Shape: Amazon Developer Hackathon (Fire TV and Alexa+ tracks, Open Source mini-challenge).

Work in progress: see CONTRACTS.md for the architecture and docs/STATUS.md for what is built.
